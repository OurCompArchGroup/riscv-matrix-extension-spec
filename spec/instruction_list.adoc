.Matrix-to-Local-Vector Reduction Instructions
[cols="^4,^2,^2,^2,^2,^2,^2,^2,^2,^4",options="header"]
|===
| format | 31:30 | 29:27 | 26 | 25 | 24:20 | 19:15 | 14:12 | 11:7 | 6:0
| vredadd.row | 10 | 000 | acc | 0 | 00000 | ms1 | 101 | vd | 0101011
| vredadd.col | 10 | 000 | acc | 1 | 00000 | ms1 | 101 | vd | 0101011
| vredmax.row | 10 | 001 | acc | 0 | 00000 | ms1 | 101 | vd | 0101011
| vredmax.col | 10 | 001 | acc | 1 | 00000 | ms1 | 101 | vd | 0101011
|===

In EW/Reduce rows, `md`, `ms1`, and accumulator `ms2` are unified matrix-register numbers 4 through 7; `vs2`, `vd`, and `vs3` are full 5-bit local-vector numbers. In reduction rows, `acc` is a one-bit immediate (0 or 1). All new instructions use the existing `+zames` feature; the optional Zmasync instruction encodings are unchanged.
