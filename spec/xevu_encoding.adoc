[[xevu-encoding]]
=== Instruction Format

All EVU instructions are 32-bit custom-0 instructions (`opcode=0x0b`),
independent of Zames custom-1. Vector register indices are five bits.

[cols="^1,^1,^1,^1,^1,^1",options="header"]
|===
| 31:25 | 24:20 | 19:15 | 14:12 | 11:7 | 6:0
| funct7 | S2 | S1 | F3 | D | 0001011
|===

.Operand fields
[cols="3,2,2,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Family | S2 | S1 | F3 | D
| Binary, reduction, gather | vs2 | vs1 | VSEW | vd
| Slide | vs2 | Offset GPR | VSEW | vd
| Unary FP math or FP/integer conversion | vs2 | 0 | VSEW | vd
| Sign XOR | vs2 | vs1 | VSEW | vd
| Narrowing immediate | vs2 | uimm5 | VSEW | vd
| Integer extension | vs2 | 0 | VSEW | vd
| Low-precision unary conversion | vs2 | 0 | 010 | vd
| MXFP conversion | vs2 | Scale vector register | 010 | vd
| Block absolute maxima | vs2 | Immediate slot 0-3 | 010 | vd
| Block pack | vs2 | Descriptor GPR | 010 | vd
| Load/store | 0 | Base GPR | VSEW | Vector data
| Gather immediate | vs2 | uimm5 | VSEW | vd
| Set immediate type | VTYPE[4:0] | AVL GPR | VTYPE[7:5] | rd
| Set register type | VTYPE GPR | AVL GPR | 111 | rd
| Fence | 0 | 0 | 000 | 0
|===

F3=000/001/010 encodes 8/16/32 bits for ordinary integer, memory, and
permutation forms. Ordinary floating-point forms allow 001/010; integer
widening allows 000/001. E0 and scalar-source forms follow their ordinary
operand layout but require F3=010 and FP32/m1. All unary forms, including
EXP2 and unary E0 forms, require S1=0. S1 in `nclip.imm`/`nsrl.imm` is a
five-bit unsigned immediate, not a vector or GPR dependency.
Configuration supplies effective width as described in <<xevu-configuration>>.

.Additional opcode-specific widths
[cols="3,2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Instruction (prefix `xv.`) | F3 / effective SEW | Input and output
| fsqrt / frsqrt7 / frec7 / fsgnjx | 001/010; 16/32 | Same-width FP16/FP32
| fsqrt.e0 / frsqrt7.e0 / frec7.e0 | 010; 32/m1 | FP32 element zero only
| fwcvt.f.f | 001; 16 | FP16 to FP32; destination EMUL=2*LMUL
| fncvt.f.f | 001; 16 | FP32 to FP16; source EMUL=2*LMUL
| fcvt.x.f / fcvt.f.x | 010; 32 | FP32 to signed INT32 / signed INT32 to FP32
| nclip.imm / nsrl.imm | 000/001; 8/16 | Source width=2*SEW, destination width=SEW
| zext2 / sext2 | 001/010; 16/32 | Source width=SEW/2, destination width=SEW
| All eighteen low-precision conversions | 010; 32/m1 | Logical value count with FP32 or packed byte/nibble/halfword operands as specified in <<xevu-low-precision>>
| block.amax / block.pack | 010; 32/m1 | Fixed 64 / 16 FP32 source values; destinations and descriptor fields follow <<xevu-block>>
|===

For low-precision conversions, F3 and the effective configuration are
e32/m1 in both directions, including BF16 and packed integer/float decoding;
they do not encode source or destination storage width.
The MXFP scale register contributes only its element-zero low byte, an
UE8M0 exponent code, not a scalar GPR or an E4M3 block scale. These eighteen
instructions use the custom-0 encodings in this appendix. They leave global
VL/VTYPE unchanged and do not use a scalar rounding-mode register.

Unused fields are zero in the documented encoding; their nonzero values
have no assigned semantics. There is no mask or programmable-stride field.
Malformed encodings, illegal widths/groups, and disabled contexts remain
illegal at VL=0; unsupported VTYPE requests instead use VILL. Unassigned
funct7 values in <<xevu-encoding-list>> are reserved and must never issue
data-memory requests. Fault delivery follows <<xevu-faults>>.
